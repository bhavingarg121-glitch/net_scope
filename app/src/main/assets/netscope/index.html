NetScope Privacy & Data Policy

Local-First Privacy: Your network telemetry stays on your device. NetScope never sells or transmits private traffic logs to third-party ad trackers.

  • Public IP & ASN: To identify your ISP gateway, Autonomous System, and regional routing node.

All test history and audit records are saved locally in your browser/device localStorage. Data is cleared automatically after 30 days or manually whenever you click below.

NetScope Scoring Methodology

The NetScope Quality Score (0–100) is calculated using a mathematically normalized, weighted composite of 6 core telemetry metrics:

Overall Score =
(0.25 × Download Score) + (0.15 × Upload Score) +
(0.20 × Latency Score) + (0.15 × Jitter Score) +
(0.10 × Signal Score) + (0.15 × Packet Stability)
